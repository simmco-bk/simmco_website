Built from creative roots. Evolved for bigger systems.

SimmCo is a strategic innovation firm founded by Bjorn K. Simmons. The firm connects people, place, capital, culture and institutions to create economic opportunity.

The company grew out of a creative and marketing foundation. That DNA still matters: strategy without imagination is incomplete, and creative without a system rarely scales. SimmCo brings both together.

The original Simmons & Company identity remains part of the firm's heritage. The sage palette carries forward as a signature accent while the primary SimmCo identity becomes bolder, more flexible and more institutional.
